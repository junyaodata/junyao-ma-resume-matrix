# Resume Defense Guide

## 60-Second Resume-Matrix Explanation

I maintain one evidence-backed master resume containing validated summary options, experience bullet sets, projects, and skills. For each application, I compare the job description with that inventory, select the most relevant evidence, remove unrelated content, and compress the result into a targeted one-page resume. I do not add unsupported claims during tailoring. I also keep an evidence ledger so I can explain the source, method, and limitations behind every important number.

## Core Interview Questions

### Why use a master resume?

It prevents useful details from being lost and makes tailoring consistent. It also separates evidence collection from last-minute application writing.

### Why is each targeted resume deletion-only?

The rule prevents unsupported claims from being invented for a specific job. If a new experience or result is real, it must first be documented and validated in the master inventory.

### How did AI contribute?

AI helped organize evidence, compare alternative wording, identify missing support, and simulate follow-up questions. I retained responsibility for factual accuracy, selection decisions, and explaining the underlying work.

### What is your strongest data-quality example?

Use either ICBC or Qulian. State the source data, validation method, exception workflow, stakeholders, and measurable result. Do not recite a bullet without explaining the process.

### What is your strongest technical project?

For analytics roles, use the Shiny CO2 dashboard. For quantitative roles, use the A-share volume-factor research. Explain the question, data, method, result, limitation, and next improvement.

## Project Answer Structure

1. Problem and user.
2. Data and unit of analysis.
3. Personal responsibility.
4. Method and key decision.
5. Result or usable output.
6. Limitation.
7. Next improvement.

## High-Risk Claims to Practice

- ICBC reported 100% accuracy.
- ICBC 10% turnaround improvement.
- ICBC 15% risk-flagging improvement.
- Qulian 5% error reduction.
- Alibaba 100% satisfaction.

If the baseline or measurement cannot be explained, replace the percentage with a concrete, non-quantified process outcome.
